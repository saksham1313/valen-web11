Unlimited routines, free for everyone

Plenty of apps put a small cap on how many routines you can save and then ask you to upgrade. Valen does not. Build a push, pull and legs split. Build a separate upper and lower rotation for when you are busy. Save a 30 minute travel session and a home version with a single pair of dumbbells. Keep old routines and swap between blocks whenever you want.

Routines can be picked into your weekly plan, so the plan and the routines you have built are connected rather than separate lists. Saved routines, like everything on the free plan, work offline and need no account.

Choose from 200+ exercises without guessing

The library covers 29 chest exercises, 28 back, 28 shoulders, 33 arms, 45 legs, 25 core and 21 cardio movements. Every exercise is mapped to the specific subzones it loads. Search by name or muscle and you see at a glance what a movement actually trains, so an incline press is labelled as an upper chest movement rather than just "chest".

Varying an exercise: warm-up, drop and failure sets

Most routine builders give you "3 x 10" and stop there. Real training has more texture. Valen lets you add three kinds of set to any rep-based lifting exercise, directly in the routine editor. The controls stay collapsed until you want them, so a simple routine stays simple.

Set typeWhat it isHow Valen counts it
Warm-upLighter preparation sets before your working weightNot counted as training volume, fatigue, PRs, e1RM or progression, so it never distorts your numbers
Drop setReduce the weight and continue right after a setCounts as real training volume at half a set of weekly credit, but its lighter load is not treated as a strength reading, so it never becomes a fake PR
FailureThe last set taken to failureCounts like a normal set and is logged as RPE 10

Up to 5 warm-up and 5 drop sets per exercise. Bodyweight exercises can have warm-up and failure sets, and a pure bodyweight set cannot be a drop set because there is no load to drop.

The credit for drop sets deserves a note. Research on drop-set training generally finds hypertrophy similar to straight sets in less time, which is why each drop is worth a partial set of weekly volume in Valen instead of a full one. It is a modelling choice, not a settled law, and it is one reason your weekly volume numbers stay honest.

Logging is quick because the routine already knows what you are doing

Start a routine and the exercises, sets and reps are already lined up. Warm-up sets are marked so they do not count toward your work. Weights are pre-filled from what you did last time, and the rest timer starts when you log a set. You mostly confirm rather than type.

When you do not have the energy for that, Quick Log is the fallback. Choose the muscle groups you trained, set the number of sets, and save. Your recovery map updates the same way. Both paths feed the same fatigue model, so you never have to choose between convenience and accurate recovery data.

A simple way to start

Build one routine for the next session you will actually do, not the perfect programme. Add four or five exercises from the library, put a warm-up set on the first heavy lift, and save it. Adjust as you learn what you like. Because there is no routine limit, nothing you save gets in the way of the next one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create unlimited workout routines in Valen?

Yes. Valen has no cap on saved routines, and it is free for every user, not only Pro subscribers.

How many exercises does Valen have?

Valen's library has 209 exercises across chest, back, shoulders, arms, legs, core and cardio. Each one is mapped to the specific muscle subzones it trains.

Can I add warm-up sets and drop sets to a routine?

Yes. In the routine editor you can add up to 5 warm-up sets and 5 drop sets to a lifting exercise, and mark the last set as taken to failure. Warm-ups are excluded from volume, drop sets count at half a set of weekly volume, and failure sets count as normal sets logged at RPE 10.

What is Quick Log in Valen?

Quick Log is a fast way to record a workout without logging each exercise. You tap the muscle groups you trained, set the number of sets, and save. The sets are spread across the muscle subzones and update your recovery map.

Are Valen routines free?

Yes. Creating and saving routines, the 200+ exercise library and Quick Log are all included on the free plan. Pro adds features like the adaptive weekly planner, deep insights, cloud backup and Smart Rest.
