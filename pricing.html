Before you buy

How much does Valen Pro cost?

$24.99 USD a year or $49.99 USD once. Google Play adjusts the price for your region and shows it in local currency at checkout.

Is the free plan a trial?

No. It is permanent. You get the full recovery map, unlimited logging, the subzone exercise library and unlimited history, with no account and no payment. Fitbod, for comparison, gives a 7-day trial before it asks you to pay.

Yearly or lifetime?

Lifetime pays for itself against yearly in under two years, then costs nothing more. Most fitness apps are subscription only.

Does free limit routines, custom exercises or logging?

No. No caps on sessions, saved routines or custom exercises, and no ads. Pro adds features, not room to log.

Can I export my data without paying?

Yes. Free includes JSON export and import of your history, custom exercises and settings. It is JSON only, with no CSV and no import from other apps. Encrypted cloud backup, which needs a Google sign-in, is Pro.

What does Pro add?

The adaptive weekly plan with training blocks, the corrective session, score breakdown, strain vs recovery, split over time, training intelligence, physique trajectory, Smart Rest, home screen widgets and encrypted cloud backup. Logging and seeing your recovery stay free.

Is there an iPhone or smartwatch version?

iOS is coming soon. There is no Wear OS or Apple Watch app and no Health Connect link. Recovery comes from the sets you log.
