# Pricing, Valen

## Free
- Price: $0
- Platform: Google Play (iOS coming soon)
- Account: Not required, local storage by default
- Caps: none on logged sessions, saved routines or custom exercises. No ads.
- Includes: full recovery heatmap (front/back body, updates hour-by-hour), unlimited workout logging, exercise library with muscle-subzone mapping, daily training suggestions, rest timer with push notification, plate calculator, JSON data export and import, unlimited training history

## Pro, Yearly
- Price: $24.99/year (USD, may vary by region/store)
- Includes everything in Free, plus:
  - Adaptive weekly planner (reschedules training around real-time fatigue, not a fixed split)
  - Deep muscle insights: subzone drill-down, 48h fatigue forecast, 7-day load trend
  - Encrypted cloud backup (optional)
  - Corrective session, score breakdown, strain vs recovery, split over time, training intelligence, physique trajectory
  - Smart Rest timer (rest time adjusts to the set)
  - Home-screen widgets

## Pro, Lifetime
- Price: $49.99 one-time (USD, may vary by region/store)
- Includes: everything in Pro Yearly, paid once instead of annually
- Note: pays for itself vs. the yearly plan in under 2 years

## Limits (stated plainly)
- No Wear OS or Apple Watch app. No Health Connect or Google Fit connection.
- Export is a JSON file only (free and Pro). No CSV export. Cannot import history from Hevy, Strong or other apps.
- Cloud backup is Pro only and needs a Google sign-in.
- Recovery is estimated from logged sets, not measured. It is not medical advice.
- Details: https://www.valenapp.tech/what-valen-does-not-do.html
